1. What this is

weftseed.com is a loom: a server that hosts accounts, each with a site at name.weftseed.com or at a domain of the person's own. It is run by one person (the operator), as a service with no promises. These terms are between you and the operator.

2. Your account

  • You must be old enough to agree to these terms where you live.
  • Give a true email address when you sign up. It is used to reach you about the account and is never shown to anyone.
  • One account for each registered domain. Keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what is done with your account.
  • Places are limited. The operator decides who is let in from the waiting list, and does not have to give a reason.

3. What you write

  • You own what you write and the pictures you upload. You give the loom permission to store it, publish it in your public feed and pages, and send it to the servers and readers that follow you. That is the whole of the permission, and it ends when you delete the post or the account.

  • You are responsible for what you publish. Do not publish what is illegal, what you have no right to publish, or what harasses or threatens people. Do not use the service to send spam, to attack other sites, or to hide the source of a message.
  • Do not use it to wear down the loom: the limits on requests, sign-in attempts and Webmentions are there for a reason, and going round them is a breach of these terms.

4. What the operator may do

  • Switch an account off (its address then answers "gone") or remove it, when these terms are broken, when the law requires it, or when your own domain stops working as described in section 6. Where it is fair to do so, the operator will write to you first.

  • Change these terms. Changes are written on this page with their date; if you do not agree with a change, you can leave.
  • Stop the service. The operator will say so beforehand where possible, and you can download your account or move it at any time before that.

5. Leaving

You can leave whenever you like. Download my data gives you everything in one file, and Move to another server carries the whole account to another loom and redirects the old address. A removed or moved account is kept for 30 days in a trash folder and then deleted. The name stays reserved after that, so nobody else can use it.

6. Your own domain

An account that signed up with a domain depends on it. If the DNS record is gone or the domain no longer reaches the loom for three daily checks in a row, you get a warning. If it is not put right within 90 days, the account is removed as in section 5. Moving or downloading before then avoids that.
