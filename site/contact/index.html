Contact

One person runs this loom. Write to the operator (turn on JavaScript to see the address).

Good reasons to write

  • A question about an account, or a problem with one.
  • Something on a site here that breaks the terms: say which address, and what is wrong.
  • A question or request about your data (see privacy).
  • A security problem: please write before you tell anyone else, and give me a chance to fix it.

Please say

Your account name (or the address of the page), and what happened. Never send a password. I will not ask for one.

Answers can take a few days.
