Questions

What does it cost?

There is no price set yet. The places on this loom are limited, and the page says how many are left. Running your own loom is free: Weft is one program.

Why do I need a domain, or a waiting list?

Spam. A domain costs a little and cannot be made by the thousand, so it keeps the loom to people who mean it. If you have no domain, ask on the waiting list: we look at where you already write, and let people in by hand, within the number of places. There is no guarantee that an account will be issued.

What can I write, and how much?

Posts in Markdown, replies, likes and pictures. Each person may store 200 MB of pictures; one picture may be up to 5 MB (25 MB if it is sent at the Compact size and made smaller). Pages show smaller copies, so a big picture does not slow a reader down. These numbers are the loom's settings and may change; the page that lists your images shows what you have left.

Who can read my posts?

Everyone. A post is published in a public feed and on a public page, like a blog. There are no private posts and no followers-only posts.

Can I follow people who are not on Weft?

Yes. Give any site's address; if it has an RSS or Atom feed, Weft finds it. Replying and liking work with sites that accept Webmention, which many blogs do.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Download my data on your profile gives one zip with your posts, replies, likes, follows and login (without sessions or API token). It is a SQLite file inside a zip, and the reference server can read it back.

Can I move to another server?

Yes. Make an empty account with the same name on the new server. On this one, open Profile, Move to another server, give your password and the new server, and you get a move code. On the new server, Move here from another server asks for that code. Your whole account is copied, and the old address redirects to the new one. The old copy waits 30 days in a trash folder, in case you change your mind.

How do I set up my own domain, and can I use Cloudflare?

Use a subdomain such as weft.yourdomain.com. Add two records at your DNS provider: a TXT record that shows the domain is yours (the profile page gives the name and the value, each with a Copy button), and a CNAME that points the name at the loom's host name (also shown there). When the domain reaches the loom, it becomes your address and you sign in again there. Do this first with the CNAME as a plain DNS record. If you then want Cloudflare in front of it, turn on its proxy (the orange cloud) and set SSL/TLS to Full (strict) for your domain. Do not use Flexible: it talks plain HTTP to the loom, which sends it back to HTTPS, and your visitors see “too many redirects”.

What if my domain stops working?

Once a day we check that the DNS record is still there and the domain still reaches the loom. After three failed checks in a row you see a warning on your profile and on your own site. You then have 90 days to put it right. After that the account is removed: it answers "gone", its file waits 30 days in the trash, and the name stays taken. You can leave before that with a move or a download. Accounts let in from the waiting list have no domain to lose.

What happens when an account is deleted?

Its address answers "gone" (HTTP 410). The file is kept for 30 days in a trash folder, so the operator can bring it back, then it is deleted for good. The name stays taken, so nobody else can speak as you at the old address.

Do you send me email?

Not yet. Email is optional on an account. When the loom can send mail, it will be used for a few things only: a code for moving, a password reset, and a warning about a domain that stopped working. No newsletters and no tracking.

Who is behind this?

One person runs it. It is not a company. The contact page has the address.

Where is the source?

On GitHub. The feed elements are specified at weftprotocol.org.
